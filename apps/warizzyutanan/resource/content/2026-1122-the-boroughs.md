## My Thoughts (Spoilers)

1. Overall it was ok and fun. Watching smart old people working together was new to me — it felt kinda like Stranger Things but with the opposite age group.
2. The ending was ok but not that touching for me, and the mystery reveal was not bad.
3. One thing I noticed: watching the older cast didn't feel as good as watching younger people. Maybe because it worried me that one day I'll have to be like that — for sure, unavoidable.
4. There's a scene where Sam says people like him or befriend him because of his wife. That resonates with me — without my wife, I wouldn't have nearly the social life or friends I have now.
