<summary><strong>Plot Summary (Spoilers)</strong></summary>

<ol>
<li id="plot-1"> Floor 4, the Iron Tangle: a knot of the world's subway systems tied together — up is down, the exit is always a few stops away.</li>
<li id="plot-2"> Donut takes the Hooligan class; the party chains their rooms into one shared base and crowdsources route info from other crawlers.</li>
<li id="plot-3"> Carl wins the seemingly-useless Dungeon Anarchist's Cookbook at the Prize Carousel — a junk-item book that secretly carries inherited notes from earlier seasons' crawlers: escape plans, system exploits, ways to undermine the dungeon itself. It's invisible to viewers and showrunners, and Carl permanently loses access if he ever reveals what it is.</li>
<li id="plot-4"> Studying it in secret, he works out the floor's engine cars, employee routes, and the Abyss, where old trains and mobs get dumped.</li>
<li id="plot-5"> Hekla's "rescue alliance" is a trap: she shoots Katia with phased bolts meant to disguise a later death. Katia's Rush accidentally kills Hekla instead.</li>
<li id="plot-6"> Katia inherits Hekla's bounty and crossbow; Brynhild's Daughters fracture.</li>
<li id="plot-7"> The party keeps rescuing stranded crawlers anyway — portal-cart runs move 1,400+ of them to the stairwells.</li>
<li id="plot-8"> The floor's designed endgame: the stairwell stations open and monster waves pour in from below. The party diverts them into the Abyss with a pre-set portal cart. To finish the remaining generators, Carl summons Grull — a war god sponsored by Prince Maestro — who kills the city-boss Mimic, then hunts Carl through a sealed trainyard in a Death Challenge until the party banishes him to the Abyss.</li>
<li id="plot-9"> The ghoul generators — soul-crystal-powered machines churning out the endless Festering Ghoul floods — are blown on purpose (a plan hatched with Prepotente's team on the other side of the tangle), ending the spawn and saving the remaining crawlers. Floor over — and in his Cookbook entry, Carl commits to going after the people running the show, not just surviving it.</li>
<li id="plot-10"> Epilogue: Loita replaces Zev as PR; Odette admits she sponsored Hekla; Donut draws the next floor — the Air Quadrant.</li>
</ol>

## My Thoughts (Spoilers)

1. Pure fun. The moment I finished it, I wanted to start the next book immediately.
2. What worked for me: the characters, the story, and the RPG elements.
3. _"the secret book"_ — I like the concept of the Cookbook: a junk prize that's secretly a hand-me-down manual for undermining the dungeon.
4. _"the betrayal twist"_ — Fun and exciting, and it ends with a satisfying twist.
5. _"the crawler rescue"_ — Heartwarming.

## Characters

New faces:

- Loita — the new PR agent; replaces Zev, who's sent to a "reeducation retreat."
- Grull — level-250 war god, sponsored by Prince Maestro; the floor's final boss.
- Frank Q — returns to hand Carl the Ring of Divine Suffering, and the truth about his wife Maggie.

Returning cast:

- Donut — Hooligan class now; spots Hekla's attack on Katia and opens fire without a second of hesitation.
- Katia — the standout: she proposes reshaping herself into an armored cowcatcher for the rescue train, then survives Hekla's betrayal and comes out with her bounty.
- Hekla — crosses the line from rival to outright villain; her plan to provoke Carl into a killable offense gets her killed instead.
- Mordecai — benched for part of the floor (attacking the Prize Carousel host), but his Special Brew saves Carl in the Death Challenge.
