<summary><strong>Plot Summary (Spoilers)</strong></summary>
<ul>
  <li>Hyeokjun, 140 million won in debt, finds a Spider Staffing Agency sticker offering part-time jobs at 50–200x the minimum wage. The catch: survive the shift, never skip one, never tell anyone what you saw.</li>
  <li>Ep 1 — convenience store night shift: a notebook of operating rules, a rat-monster coworker, and a woman-faced insect creature locked in the storeroom fridge. He survives until the 6 a.m. sunlight.</li>
  <li>Ep 2 — a pharmaceutical event staffed with a non-human "Cheerful Pierrot"; the show ends in explosions and slaughter, and the Pierrot gestures at him to smile.</li>
  <li>Ep 3 — a 200x-pay milk delivery in a haunted building where every door reads 444; Hyeokjun is dragged into a white void and texts his sister to go to Spider Staffing.</li>
  <li>Ep 4–5 — his sister Jiyun takes a haunted movie theater job to find him; both cross to the other world and escape a clinical trial where the danger was in the water, not the pills.</li>
  <li>Their father vanished years ago after the same sequence of "honey jobs" — his notebook matches every offer Hyeokjun got.</li>
  <li>Ep 6 — a construction site hides a tower of worker corpses; the foreman admits Hyeokjun's father went through a door to the other world and never came back.</li>
  <li>Ep 7 — Yeonju, the "workplace accident investigator," is revealed to be a 1980s factory worker who crossed over; the door back is at Neo World amusement park, where the night parade's animal costumes fuse to workers' bodies.</li>
  <li>Ep 8 — the agency boss shoots Yeonju, declaring the world needs sacrifices; black beings drag him and her through the door. Beyond it, Hyeokjun's father — half monster — presses money into his hand and pushes him out.</li>
  <li>The siblings shut the door, but the black smoke escapes. Debt-free, Hyeokjun saves a jumper on a bridge — then gets a text: he's hired for management at Spider Staffing. His answer is never shown.</li>
</ul>
</details>

## My Thoughts

- Meh. The mystery part wasn't good enough for me.
- The overall vibe was too cartoonish, and the acting was overdramatic.
- What kept us watching to the end was wanting to know what the mystery was actually about.
- The short 30–40 minute episodes made it easy to finish.
