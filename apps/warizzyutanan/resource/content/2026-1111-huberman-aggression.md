## Summary

- Three types of aggression — reactive, proactive, indirect — each with its own biology.
- Aggression is a build-up like hydraulic pressure, not one event. "Aggression is just sadness" is false — different circuits.
- ~3,000 neurons in the **ventromedial hypothalamus (VMH)** are necessary and sufficient for aggression. Stimulate them: instant rage. Stop: calm in seconds.
- **Estrogen, not testosterone, triggers aggression.** Testosterone must convert to estrogen in the brain first. It just amplifies what you already are.
- Estrogen raises aggression only in **short days** (high cortisol, low dopamine/serotonin). Long sunny days: no effect.

## Tools

- Get sunlight in your eyes early in the day and as much as safely possible throughout.
- Sauna (20 min at 80–100 °C) or hot baths lower cortisol.
- Ashwagandha potently lowers cortisol — but only ~2 weeks on, ~2 weeks off, and check with a doctor first.
- Acetyl-L-carnitine reduced aggressive behavior, attention problems, and delinquency in kids with ADHD (randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled crossover trial).
- No single fix — stack sunlight, stress management, diet, and targeted supplements to lower the pressure.
