<summary><strong>Plot Summary (Spoilers)</strong></summary>

<ul>
<li>Yui, 40, treats saving money as her one hobby. A free cancer screening finds uterine cancer — about a year to live if untreated. She refuses treatment and feels relief: no reason to save anymore.</li>
<li>At the hospital, a pink-haired host named Sena begs her to lend him ¥720,000 for his father's hospital fees. Instead, she "buys" him: the money in exchange for 70 hours of company.</li>
<li>The 70 hours fund her first-ever luxuries — premium cinema seats, a hot-spring trip, department-store food. But on her birthday his niece eats her cake, and she ends the contract.</li>
<li>At Christmas she visits his club and proposes marriage: stay with me until I die, inherit everything. They register the marriage.</li>
<li>Family visits go badly. Yui won't tell her family about the cancer, and Sena's sister Nachi calls her "the worst" for controlling people with money. When Sena finds out she has refused all treatment, they split.</li>
<li>Yui collapses at home in severe pain. Sena runs there, calls an ambulance, and says he simply doesn't want her to die. She finally accepts treatment — a hysterectomy, then chemo.</li>
<li>Before surgery she hands him signed divorce papers, so he won't be tied down. He never files them.</li>
<li>Three years later: no recurrence, still married. Sena runs a food truck with his sister, and Yui re-tucks a quilt over him as he sleeps — the film's quiet answer to the stepmother who once threw her mother's quilt away.</li>
</ul>

## My Thoughts (Spoilers)

- Kinda meh. I don't mind a rom-com following the formula — this one just didn't follow it well. Sena's character development never stood out for me.
- The one thing I liked was the casting of Yui. She looks like a real 40-year-old woman, not conventionally beautiful. That fits the character — someone a bit antisocial with no friends shouldn't look glamorous.
- Her character isn't flawless either, which I appreciated. She even had an affair with a married coworker in the past.
- I also liked that the film never shows the sex scenes. It just treats them as a normal part of life.
