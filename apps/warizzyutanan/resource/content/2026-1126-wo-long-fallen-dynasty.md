## My Thoughts (Spoilers)

1. The combat system was the highlight — fast, fun, and super satisfying, and the difficulty hit a good middle ground: not too hard, not too easy. It carried the rating.
2. Once I found a build that suited me I breezed through the game to the end without much effort, and I didn't try any other build because I was too lazy to set one up.
3. I skipped all of the story — I don't even know how the protagonist fits into it — but I'm okay with that; it was very good that the game lets you skip everything when you only care about the gameplay.
4. The looting system was a bit disappointing: you can pick one rank-4 armor or weapon and use it for the entire game, since you can change the equipment's attributes at will, so RNG barely affects you — which I think is somewhat good and bad.
5. The platinum trophy felt satisfying, but not much more since it took a short time and wasn't that hard.
