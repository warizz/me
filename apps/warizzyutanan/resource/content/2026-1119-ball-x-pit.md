<summary><strong>Plot Summary (Spoilers)</strong></summary>

<ol>
  <li id="plot-1">Ballbylon, a great city, is annihilated by a "meteoric and completely unexpected event." All that remains is an ominous, bottomless pit.</li>
  <li id="plot-2">Treasure hunters flock to the ruins and settle beside the pit, riding the Pit Lift down to plunder the city's lost riches. Few return.</li>
  <li id="plot-3">Between dives, survivors rebuild New Ballbylon on the surface; deeper lift layers are unlocked with Gears from first boss clears.</li>
  <li id="plot-4">The eight layers escalate from cartoon undead (The BONExYARD) through desert, fungal forest, grasslands, and infernal depths up to actual angels at The HEAVENLYxGATES.</li>
  <li id="plot-5">The bottom layer, The VASTxVOID, is the surface of the Moon itself, dotted with ruined architecture and sphere-shaped Moonlings.</li>
  <li id="plot-6">The final boss is The Moon — a sentient, eight-eyed celestial entity. The "meteor" that destroyed Ballbylon was The Moon plunging through the city and punching all the way through the planet, creating the pit.</li>
  <li id="plot-7">Each character that defeats The Moon opens one of its eight doors. After eight different characters win, the Moon expels technicolor waves of every enemy in the game and rises from the pit as a restored, technicolor Ballbylon.</li>

## My Thoughts (Spoilers)

1. The mechanics are so satisfying — bouncing, catching, fusing balls. It hooked me right away.
2. The base building between runs is a bit of a slog. It didn't ruin the game for me, but it's the weak half of the loop.
3. The platinum grind got tedious, but luckily the grindiest trophies can be done with the auto-play character — set it up and AFK.
4. I love the music. Glad it's not just one track per stage — each stage's theme deserves more room.
5. I liked the vibe — it starts goofy and ends in mystery and cosmic horror vibes, and that contrast really works.
6. Got the platinum with zero clue about the story — looked it up afterward and I like that too.
