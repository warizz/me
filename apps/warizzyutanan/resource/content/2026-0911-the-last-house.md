## My Thoughts (Spoilers)

- Liked the premise of people being trapped in a house with no internet and no way to go outside.
- The premise made me think about how people would survive and stay connected in that situation.
- The reveal that the monsters might come from the sea was interesting.
- Found the action and chase scenes boring, but some of the family scenes were interesting.
