Thailand's social security fund — ฿2.8 trillion covering 24 million people — is run by a 21-member board: 7 government, 7 employer representatives, 7 insured-person representatives. Only the 14 employer and insured seats are elected. The first direct election was 2023; this is the second. Candidates run nationwide, voters pick up to 7 names on one side.

### Jan — the 'engrave it in your skull' chat leak

The fire that lit the election year:

- Jan 22: SSO holds a press conference defending the fund's investment in the TU Dome property after widespread criticism — [Siam News](https://www.siamnews.com/news/economy/36971)
- Jan 23–24: insured-side board member Sassatham posts that "while the insured were furious, the board's Line group was cheering each other on and cursing the 'unlucky media'"; TV host Sirot Klamphaiboon publishes the leaked chat — one member wrote the fund's ฿80bn+ investment gains should make people "engrave it in your skull", another "the media are so unlucky" — [Siam News](https://www.siamnews.com/news/economy/36971) · [Daily News](https://www.dailynews.co.th/news/5531504/)
- The full leaked message: "ฟังทีมเลขาแถลงแล้ว ถือครบถ้วนกระบวนการ ละเอียดดีแล้ว แล้วฟังคนที่คาบข่าวไปให้ผู้อื่นออกหาเสียงในช่วงนี้ จะอธิบายละเอียดอย่างไร..? เขาก็เอาคำพูดของพวกเราไปตอกย้ำ เล่นงาน สปส. เงินกำไรที่ลงทุน 8 หมื่นกว่าให้พวกเขาจำใส่กะโหลกเสียบ้าง..!" — [Siam News](https://www.siamnews.com/news/economy/36971)
- And the encouragement it got: "พี่ให้กำลังใจน้อง…มากๆๆๆ ครับ สื่อเฮงซวยยยยยย" ("big brother cheering the team on… the media are so damn unlucky") — replied to with "ขอบคุณในกำลังใจที่มอบให้ผมครับ" — [Siam News](https://www.siamnews.com/news/economy/36971) · [Daily News](https://www.dailynews.co.th/news/5531504/)
- The only board member whose name fits the initials in the chat: Thaweekiat Rotsawasut, 80, the sole employer-side representative at the time — chairman of the Employers' Confederation of Thailand, with business interests in labour supply and security services — [Daily News](https://www.dailynews.co.th/news/5534828/) · [Bangkok Biz News](https://www.bangkokbiznews.com/politics/1218117)

_Thaweekiat Rotsawasut, the man the chat pointed to. Photo: [Daily News](https://www.dailynews.co.th/news/5534828/)_
- Jan 27: Thaweekiat denies demanding gratitude, says the quote was a private reply to a reporter, and tells the press to "ask Sassatham" where the leak came from — [Bright TV](https://www.brighttv.co.th/news/drama-line) · [PPTV](https://www.pptvhd36.com/video/news/210312)
- Feb 2: he releases the full chat, saying the leaks were "cut out of context to mislead", and apologises "for the discomfort" — [CEO Vision](https://ceovision.asia/?p=7510)
- He ran for re-election in Oct as leader of the SSD team. He lost — [Matichon](https://www.matichon.co.th/local/quality-life/news_5798727)

### Jun–Sep — the old guard fights back

Phanat Thai Luan, 84, former board member for 8 terms (all appointed, pre-2023), chairman of a council of 286 labour unions, running employer-side:

- Jun 4: defends the fund's spending on Labour Day shirts, caps and calendars against "it's our money" critics — the same ~฿50M/year calendar budget the leaked-chat reporting flagged — [Khao Sod](https://www.khaosod.co.th/special-stories/news_10269912) · [Daily News](https://www.dailynews.co.th/news/5531504/)
- Jul 9: posts that he will "storm every factory of our 287 unions" to stop the "puppets" — calling the Progressive team hirelings of a political party out to swallow the fund — [Daily News](https://www.dailynews.co.th/news/6014069/) · [The Thaiger](https://thethaiger.com/th/news/1586053/)
- Sep 25: on TV, "1,000-million-percent confident" of winning — his union network alone could turn out half a million votes; workers would "chant the numbers 63–69" of his team before voting — [Khao Sod](https://www.khaosod.co.th/special-stories/news_10413201)

### Aug–Sep — 180 candidates, 5 teams

- Official candidate list signed Aug 18, published Aug 19: 102 insured-side, 78 employer-side — [Boonarayapon](https://boonarayapon.com/blog/sso-180-candidates-revealed-2569/)
- Insured side: 5 declared teams (35 people) — Progressive Social Security (no. 41–47, led by Sassatham Thammabutdi, the 2023 winner), Clear SSO, SSIA, Labour Power, plus ~67 independents — [Matichon](https://www.matichon.co.th/local/quality-life/news_5798727) · [Boonarayapon](https://boonarayapon.com/blog/sso-all-teams-comparison-2569/)
- Campaign stakes: investment transparency, the CARE pension formula (court decree 3307/2567), benefits, IT budget — [The Standard](https://thestandard.co/sso-board-election-candidates/) · [Matichon](https://www.matichon.co.th/local/quality-life/news_5798727)

### Sat 26 Sep — postponed by the floods

One day before vote day, the election committee moved the vote from Sep 27 to Oct 11 — flash floods and runoff in many provinces threatened travel, ballot boxes and count logistics; Thailand is a single constituency, so the whole country moves together. — [Thai PBS](https://www.thaipbs.or.th/news/content/558623)

### Sun 11 Oct — vote day

- Phanat, in a wheelchair, casts his ballot in Samut Prakan: "I'm not here to beat the puppets — I'm here to beat the party behind them… they'd have to take my blood first" — [The Standard](https://thestandard.co/panat-social-security-election-political/) · [The Key](https://thekey.news/news/politics/66334/)
- 5,233 polling units (up from ~2,000 in 2023), ~47,000 staff, no pre-registration required this time; ~11–12 million eligible — [The Reporters](https://www.thereporters.co/tw-politics/1010261234/) · [Spacebar](https://editors.spacebar.th/business/sso-board-election-2569)
- Turnout: ~672,807 insured (+331% vs 2023's ~150,000) and ~9,000 employers (+512%) — [Nation TV](https://www.nationtv.tv/news/current-issue/378984687) · [The Standard](https://thestandard.co/social-security-board-election-5/)
- 3 polling spots had over 200,000 eligible each (Silom Complex, JJ Mall, Samut Prakan); traffic jams from early morning; minor issues — wrong polling place, lapsed contributions — resolved case by case — [Thai PBS](https://www.thaipbs.or.th/news/content/570839) · [The Standard](https://thestandard.co/social-security-board-election-5/)

### Sun 11 Oct, night — the count

- SSO opened a real-time dashboard at sso.go.th covering all 5,233 units; unofficial results expected ~20:00 but counting ran through the night — [Bangkok Biz News](https://www.bangkokbiznews.com/economics/labour/1255919) · [Thai PBS](https://www.thaipbs.or.th/news/content/570840)
- Progressive Social Security team leads all 7 employer seats and (with one independent, no. 38 Lakshmi Suwannaphakdi) the insured top-7 — Phanat's team 63–69 and Thaweekiat's SSD win nothing — [TNN](https://www.tnnthailand.com/social/249432/) · [The Active](https://theactive.thaipbs.or.th/news/welfare-20261011-2)
- Team leader Sassatham declared victory at 21:30 with only ~15–20% counted, thanking "ordinary people… many crossed traffic jams and floods" — first-7-days pledges: admin budget ≤3%, IT budget ≤300M, live-streamed board meetings, CARE pension formula — [Nation TV](https://www.nationtv.tv/news/current-issue/378984696)

![Progressive Social Security team victory announcement](/posts/2026-1011-sso-board-election-05-progressive-victory.webp "2026-10-11")
_"ประกันสังคมก้าวหน้า คว้าชัย" — the Progressive team claims victory on election night. Photo: [Nation TV](https://www.nationtv.tv/news/current-issue/378984696)_
- Count complaints: Bang Phli (Samut Prakan) units with missing/zero scores and officials gone home; Ratchaburi recounted after team votes vanished — one candidate went from 1 to 80 votes — [Nation TV](https://www.nationtv.tv/news/current-issue/378984696)

### Mon 12 Oct — ~88% counted

- 5,053,975 votes counted; employer side 88.28%, insured side 87.55%; Progressive team still sweeping both boards (unofficial) — [PPTV](https://www.pptvhd36.com/news/สังคม/285056)
- SSO secretary-general: official certification targeted within the year — the 2023 cycle took 70 days, this one has ~4.5× the ballots; complaints window is 7 days after winners are posted — [Thai PBS](https://www.thaipbs.or.th/news/content/570839) · [Bangkok Biz News](https://www.bangkokbiznews.com/economics/labour/1255919)
